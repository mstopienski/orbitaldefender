OrbitalDefender

Defend an orbital station with two turrets that ride on rails. Waves of ships, bosses, and upgrades bought with crystals.

Google Play – coming soon Screenshots

How it plays

Hold your phone upright. The left half of the screen controls the left turret and the right half controls the right one, so you steer both with your thumbs at the same time. Your finger sets the turret's position along its rail, and the barrel tilts toward the center on its own.

After every level you spend crystals in the shop: faster cooling, shorter charge time, longer range, an extra barrel and stronger bonus attacks. Ships get tougher as you go, so choose your upgrades wisely.

Four bonus attacks

  • NukeA blast ring expands from the station and hits every enemy it reaches.
  • LaserA continuous beam from a turret that pierces every enemy along the line of fire.
  • Missile barrageA volley of missiles, each one exploding in an area.
  • ShieldProtects the station from hits, but every hit shortens how long it lasts.

Enemies

The game starts with simple corvettes. New ship types show up gradually, and every 10 levels a boss arrives after the regular wave.

  • Corvettefrom level 1
  • Scoutfrom level 10
  • Mantafrom level 15
  • Interceptorfrom level 20
  • Heavy Interceptorfrom level 25
  • Cruiserfrom level 30
  • Carrierfrom level 40
  • Dreadnoughtfrom level 50
  • Flagshipfrom level 60
  • Dominator (boss)every 10 levels
