About the studio

Sibox Game Studio is an independent studio that makes mobile games.

What we do

We make games for phones, from the first idea through art and sound to release in the store. We favor short, readable mechanics and upgrades that really change how you play.

Our first game is OrbitalDefender. The next project, Rift Runners, is in development.

How we work

We build our games in Godot 4. Difficulty and rewards are tuned with an economy simulation and a bot that plays through the levels.
