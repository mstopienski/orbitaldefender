Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the OrbitalDefender app for Android uses and why.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who we are

OrbitalDefender (the "app") is published by Sibox Game Studio ("we", "us"), an independent mobile game studio. If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to us: contact@example.com.

Short version

  • You do not need to create an account to play. Signing in is optional and only needed for online leaderboards and seasons.
  • We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may process some device data (see below).
  • Online leaderboards and seasons use the third-party service CheddaBoards.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your progress (levels completed, crystals, upgrades, settings and language) locally on your device. We do not receive this data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, app interaction data and approximate location derived from your IP address. This may be used for personalized or non-personalized ads, fraud prevention and analytics.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information:

  • How Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Leaderboards and seasons (CheddaBoards)

The app uses CheddaBoards, a third-party leaderboard service, for online leaderboards and seasons. Playing the game does not require an account. Signing in is optional and is only needed if you want to take part in online leaderboards and seasons.

Depending on how you use the feature, CheddaBoards may process:

  • an anonymous device identifier, used to recognise your device on the leaderboard;
  • a player name: one is generated automatically, and you can choose your own. It may be shown publicly on the leaderboard, so please do not use your real name or other personal information;
  • your scores, level progress and rankings submitted to the leaderboard;
  • if you choose to sign in, basic account details provided by the sign-in provider (Google or Apple), such as an account identifier.

Season rewards (for example crystals) are granted based on your leaderboard ranking.

If you play without signing in, your scores are not submitted to the online season leaderboards. CheddaBoards processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. See the CheddaBoards website for its privacy information. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider's privacy policy also applies (Google, Apple).

To have your leaderboard data removed, contact us at the e-mail address above and include your player name.

Google Play and purchases

Downloading the app through Google Play is handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the third-party services described above (Google AdMob and CheddaBoards, and Google or Apple if you choose to sign in with them), to the extent needed for them to work.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its processing and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. We do not keep an account or personal profile for you ourselves; leaderboard data is held by CheddaBoards and advertising data by Google. You can exercise your rights by contacting us, and for advertising data also through your Android settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
